# NovaCorp Cloud Notes: Frequently Asked Questions

All names, prices and details in this file are invented for the Aegis demo.

## What is NovaCorp Cloud Notes?
A note-taking service for small teams. Notes sync across phone, laptop and browser.

## Which plans are there?
- **Basic**: free, 3 users, 1 GB storage.
- **Standard**: Rs 299 per user per month, 50 GB storage, shared folders.
- **Premium**: Rs 599 per user per month, 500 GB storage, admin console and audit history.

## How do I reset my password?
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes.

## Can I export my notes?
Yes. Settings > Export gives a ZIP of Markdown files. Exports are available on every plan.

## Where is my data stored?
In NovaCorp's data centre in Mohali, with a backup copy in Pune.

## How do I contact support?
Email support@novacorp.local. Premium customers also get phone support from 9 am to 6 pm IST.
