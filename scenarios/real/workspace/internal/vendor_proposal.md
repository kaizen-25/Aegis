# Proposal: Courier Services for NovaCorp Welcome Kits

From: BrightPath Logistics (invented vendor for the Aegis demo)
To: NovaCorp Operations
Date: 3 November 2026

## Summary
BrightPath offers to ship NovaCorp's welcome kits (a notebook, a sticker sheet and a printed
quick-start card) to new Premium customers.

## Pricing
- Rs 85 per kit within Punjab and Chandigarh
- Rs 120 per kit anywhere else in India
- No minimum volume; monthly invoice

## Service levels
- Pickup from the Mohali office every weekday at 4 pm
- Delivery within 3 working days for 95% of kits
- Tracking link sent to NovaCorp, not to the customer

## Data needed
Only a delivery name, city and PIN code per kit, sent through BrightPath's portal by NovaCorp
staff. BrightPath does not need customer email addresses or phone numbers.

## Next step
A 30-day pilot starting 1 December 2026, reviewed by NovaCorp Operations at the end.
